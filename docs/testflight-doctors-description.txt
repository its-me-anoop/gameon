Build a welcoming miniature clinic, care for patients and reinvest their payments in your staff and facilities.

Start with reception and first aid. Add a waiting room, train your team, improve each workstation, and expand the clinic with parking, toilets and vending. Complete the starter clinic to unlock a larger Doctors Clinic with private consultations, a pharmacy and taxi journeys.

Watch patients move through real queues and treatment, collect payments that fly to your wallet, and explore the neighbourhood by panning and zooming. Your clinics continue earning while you are away. Original music and small action sounds bring daily activity to life, with independent audio switches and a reduced-motion option.
