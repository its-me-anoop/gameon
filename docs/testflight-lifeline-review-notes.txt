Little Lifeline is the 3.0 redesign of this existing app as a Unity 3D hospital-on-rails management game. The bundle identifier remains com.flutterly.gravitile. No app-specific account is required. Game Center sign-in is optional and begins only after a player requests rankings.

On a fresh installation, tap Open clinic. Residents arrive on the platform and the doctor begins consultation automatically. Tap a carriage to inspect or improve it, or choose an empty carriage slot to add consultation, diagnostics or recovery. Use the people icon along the bottom to assign and train crew. The campaign includes three towns, four carriage slots and a roster of up to six crew members. The bottom map icon opens Route, town projects and travel. The configured hospital earns progress while away for up to 24 hours; the return report describes progress already saved.

The trophy icon along the bottom opens The Weekly Call, a fixed four-minute scenario with the same starting resources and deterministic arrivals for the current UTC week. Planning and pausing are free. Rankings prioritize completed visits, with shorter waiting breaking ties. The leaderboard identifier is grv3.lifeline.weekly.v1. Its first occurrence opens on 14 September 2026 at 00:00 UTC, then repeats every Monday for seven days. Before that opening, the scenario is available to practise locally and the rankings control explains the opening date. Pre-opening and expired-week results never enter a later occurrence. Old arcade boards and their pending scores are not used.

The gear at the top right opens the depot. Choose its palette icon (Finishes) to preview the cosmetic collection. The one-time Founder’s Carriage Collection uses the existing non-consumable com.flutterly.gravitile.plus and grants Sunrise, Coastal and Heritage train finishes. Verified previous Plus purchases also unlock the collection. The entire campaign and weekly challenge are free; paid finishes never affect income, care or score. Restore is available in both the depot and finishes controls.

The optional tip control expands three consumable tips: com.flutterly.gravitile.tip.small, com.flutterly.gravitile.tip.medium and com.flutterly.gravitile.tip.large. Tips support game development and grant no items or gameplay benefit; this is not a charitable donation flow. There are no subscriptions, ads or tracking SDKs. Progress is local to the device. Sound, haptics and reduced motion controls are in the depot; system Reduce Motion is also respected. The depot’s help icon opens the fuller hospital guide.
