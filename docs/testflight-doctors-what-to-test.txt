Little Lifeline 3.3 — Doctors clinic and original audio

Complete every starter-clinic room, workstation, staff-training and amenity upgrade, then collect 100,000 coins to open the new Doctors Clinic from Locations.

The new location includes four receptionists, four nurses, four doctors in private consultation rooms, two pharmacists, larger waiting and toilet facilities, twelve parking spaces and a taxi stand. Staff and stations are hired and installed progressively. Rooms have six tiers and components have twelve levels, with doubled base income, prices and service/construction times.

Please try:
• Opening the new clinic, returning to the starter clinic and travelling back. The wallet follows you; each clinic keeps its own uncollected payments and patients.
• Watching paid patients proceed through consultation, first aid and pharmacy, including car and taxi arrivals and departures.
• Watching taxi passengers wait separately and walk to their called car. Reception should keep serving people inside while other visitors are still travelling to the clinic.
• Improving individual stations, training staff and renovating while the clinic remains open.
• Panning, zooming and collecting from each reception desk without accidental purchases.
• Listening to the original background score and action sounds. Music and Effects have separate switches in Settings; Less motion remains available.
• Backgrounding or restarting during care and construction, then collecting the earnings waiting at reception.

Please report unexpected backtracking, blocked doors or queues, overlapping vehicles or people, incorrect balances, missing sound, or music restarting/overlapping when travelling.
