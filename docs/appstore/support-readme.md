# Little Lifeline support

Little Lifeline is a miniature 3D clinic management game for iPhone and iPad. Start with reception and first aid, improve your team and facilities, and work toward opening a larger doctors clinic.

For private support, email [anoopjose.flutterly@gmail.com](mailto:anoopjose.flutterly@gmail.com). You can also [report a game issue](https://github.com/its-me-anoop/gravitile-support/issues). GitHub issues are public; do not include personal, payment or medical information.

[Privacy policy](privacy.md)

## Start your clinic

1. Let the first patient reach reception and pay.
2. Tap the cash at the counter to collect 50 coins.
3. Select the highlighted first-aid room and hire your first nurse.
4. Watch the first patient receive care. More patients will then arrive.

Money at reception is not yet in your wallet. Tap it to collect before making a purchase. When two paid patients are waiting, you can build the waiting room.

## Explore and upgrade

Drag to move the camera and pinch to zoom. Tap Home to return to reception. Tap a room to improve equipment, facilities and decorations, or begin a renovation. Tap an individual desk or treatment station to upgrade it or train its staff member.

Each room tier sets an upgrade limit. Renovating the room raises that limit and takes time. Current services continue during construction. Parking, toilets and vending have their own upgrade controls.

To open the doctors clinic, finish every starter-clinic upgrade and construction job, hire and install all starter staff and stations, and collect 100,000 coins. **Locations** shows the remaining requirements. You can return to the starter clinic after opening the second location. Both keep their own facilities, patients and counter cash; your collected wallet follows you.

## Patients are waiting

Watch where the queue is forming. Reception takes payment only when it can reserve a care or waiting place. A full paid queue can therefore pause check-in. Let current patients finish, improve the relevant station or train staff, and add staff or waiting capacity when available.

In the doctors clinic, a visit continues through consultation, first aid and pharmacy before departure. People arriving by car or taxi also need time to park, walk and return to their vehicle. A full transport area can lengthen these waits.

If a patient appears stuck through several service cycles, send the app version, location, device model and a screenshot or short recording. Avoid deleting the app as a first troubleshooting step: that can remove local progress.

## Music, effects and motion

Open **Settings** for separate **Music** and **Effects** switches. Check both switches and your device's volume if the game is silent. You can also change **Haptics** and **Less motion**. Essential walking remains visible with Less motion enabled.

## Saves and offline earnings

Progress is saved locally. Existing staff can earn for up to eight hours while the app is away; return to collect the money waiting at reception. Timed construction can finish during a longer absence. There is no game-account login or developer cloud-save service.

Keep the app installed when updating. Deleting it removes local game data, while offloading may retain that data. Device backups are controlled through Apple or your computer's backup settings. We cannot retrieve a lost save from a Little Lifeline account.

## Purchases

The current clinic has no shop, advertisements, subscriptions or purchases required to progress. **Restore purchases** checks earlier verified ownership through Apple. It does not grant extra coins or unlock a paid clinic benefit in this version. For an Apple transaction or refund, use [Apple's purchase support](https://support.apple.com/billing).

## Send a useful report

Include your app version and build, iPhone or iPad model, iOS/iPadOS version, the clinic you were in and what happened immediately before the problem. A screenshot or short recording can help. Do not send passwords, full payment details or real medical information.

Little Lifeline is a fictional game. It does not provide medical advice or care services.
