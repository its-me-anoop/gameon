# Little Lifeline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

Little Lifeline is a fictional clinic management game developed by Anoop Jose (Flutterly). You do not need an account to play. The game does not ask for or store real patient or medical information.

## Game data stays on your device

Your clinic progress, coins, staff, upgrades, patients, construction and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. Earlier game saves and previously verified purchase ownership may also be retained so an update does not erase them. The patients in these saves are simulated game characters.

We do not send your clinic progress or gameplay activity to a developer-operated server. We do not use advertisements, tracking or developer analytics. The game uses the Unity engine; Unity Advertising and Unity Analytics are not used by this game.

Your device may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup, depending on your Apple settings. We do not operate a cloud-save service and cannot retrieve your game progress from a developer account.

## Apple services

The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to check previously verified purchase ownership and may request App Store product information. Choosing **Restore purchases** asks Apple to synchronise eligible earlier purchases and may prompt for Apple Account authentication. This release does not offer new purchases or require payment to progress. Restoring earlier ownership does not unlock an additional clinic benefit in this version.

Apple handles its App Store services, transactions and any payment information. We do not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

The current clinic game does not use Game Center leaderboards. Device backups and any diagnostics you choose to share through Apple's system settings are managed through Apple and those settings.

## Support and external links

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device model, app version and description of the issue. We use it to respond and troubleshoot. We keep support correspondence as needed to resolve the request and maintain a support record. You can request deletion by emailing us at the address below, subject to any records we need to retain for legal obligations.

The support page and privacy policy are hosted on GitHub. Opening these links takes you to an external service, which may process ordinary web request information under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). GitHub issues are public. Do not post personal, payment or medical information in an issue. Use email for private support.

## Removing your data

Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. Offloading the app may retain that data. Copies in device backups are controlled separately through your Apple or computer backup settings. There is no Little Lifeline account to delete and no developer-held copy of your clinic save to recover.

For support correspondence, email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you previously sent. If you posted on GitHub, you can also manage your post through your GitHub account; GitHub's own retention rules apply to its service.

## Children

The same local-save design applies to all players. We do not ask children to create an account or submit personal information to play. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent us personal information through support can contact us to request deletion.

## Changes to this policy

We will publish changes here with an updated effective date. If an app update changes how personal information is handled, this policy will describe that change.
